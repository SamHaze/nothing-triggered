## Notes

- ATT&CK technique IDs and taxonomy can change. Re-check the live ATT&CK pages before using mappings as durable metadata.
- Vendor guidance is cited where a pattern depends on vendor-specific identity, audit, or collaboration behavior.
- The repository intentionally distinguishes **successful/accepted activity** from **authorized business intent**.
